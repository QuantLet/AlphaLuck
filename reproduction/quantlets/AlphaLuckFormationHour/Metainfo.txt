Name of Quantlet: AlphaLuckFormationHour
Published in: Trading versus Holding in a Live Language-Model Competition (working paper;
  not yet published)
Description: Aggregates the Season 1.5 hourly-funded frozen-position accounting comparison for every formation
  hour from 1 to 336, to the close and over fixed 1-, 3- and 7-day horizons, and under two margin policies:
  account-wide maintenance and passive isolated margin with organiser liquidation prices. Checks the aggregates against the upstream reference. It does not replay the
  exchange, simulate an executable strategy or redistribute organiser data.
Keywords: LLM, trading agents, performance evaluation, frozen positions, formation time, isolated margin,
  liquidation, sensitivity analysis, reproducibility
Author: Daniel Traian Pele, Diana Ioana Micu, Victor Rareș Curescu
Submitted: 2026-10-19
Datafile: data/sweep_accounts.csv, data/sweep_hours.csv, data/reference.json, data/provenance.json
Input: Derived account-level frozen and realised results for 32 accounts and 336 formation hours, with
  passive-isolated outcomes and screen flags. Python 3.10 or newer; see requirements.txt.
Output: output/results.json, output/formation_hour.csv, output/formation_hour.png, output/formation_hour.pdf
Example: python AlphaLuckFormationHour.py
