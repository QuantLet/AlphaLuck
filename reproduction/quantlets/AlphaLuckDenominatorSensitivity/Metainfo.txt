Name of Quantlet: AlphaLuckDenominatorSensitivity
Published in: Risk Measurement and Performance Benchmarks in a Live LLM Trading Competition (working paper)
Description: Recalculates descriptive mean-square ratios, rank concordance and risk-return correlations
  on current equity and initial capital from derived Alpha Arena account data. Common-date block reweighting
  retains all 32 accounts and both volatility definitions together while final outcomes remain fixed.
  The resulting ranges are sensitivity summaries, not confidence intervals or tests of model skill.
Keywords: risk measurement, volatility, trading, large language models, Spearman correlation, Kendall
  concordance, sensitivity analysis, resampling
Author: Daniel Traian Pele
Submitted: 15 October 2026, Daniel Traian Pele
Datafile: data/account_measures.csv, data/paired_daily_inputs.csv
Input: 'Derived Season 1.5 observations: 32 accounts, 8 models, 4 arms, and 14 daily equity changes.'
Output: AlphaLuckDenominatorSensitivity.png, AlphaLuckDenominatorSensitivity.pdf, outputs/denominator_comparison.csv,
  outputs/joint_block_summary.csv, outputs/paired_leave_one_out.csv, outputs/verification.json
