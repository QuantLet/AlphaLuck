Name of Quantlet: AlphaLuckFrozenBenchmark
Published in: Trading versus Holding in a Live Language-Model Competition (working paper;
  not yet published)
Description: Reproduces the Season 1.5 24-hour frozen-position accounting comparison from derived hourly-funded
  account endpoints and terminal quantities. Recomputes notional-based closing-cost and common endpoint-price
  stresses. It does not simulate an executable strategy, historical liquidations or market impact.
Keywords: LLM, trading agents, performance evaluation, frozen positions, funding, transaction costs, sensitivity
  analysis, reproducibility
Author: Daniel Traian Pele, Diana Ioana Micu, Victor Rareș Curescu
Submitted: 2026-10-15
Datafile: data/accounts.csv, data/positions.csv, data/provenance.json
Input: Derived hourly-funded dollar profit and loss for 32 accounts and signed terminal quantities for
  seven instruments. Python 3.10 or newer; see requirements.txt.
Output: output/results.json, output/closing_cost_sensitivity.csv, output/endpoint_price_sensitivity.csv,
  output/frozen_benchmark.png, output/frozen_benchmark.pdf
Example: python AlphaLuckFrozenBenchmark.py
