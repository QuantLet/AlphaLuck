\paragraph{Hourly funding.}
We reconstruct funding payments from the exchange's historical hourly rates as
$-q_{ik,h}P_{k,h}r_{k,h}$, using actual positions just before each nominal
UTC settlement hour and the last pooled trade price at that hour in place of
unavailable historical oracle prices. We assign each funding record to
the start of its reported hour. The official window includes 338 hourly observations
for each of seven instruments. Daily aggregates of the reconstructed actual
payments have correlation 0.999998 with recorded cash flows and mean absolute
error of \$0.0052 across 2,558 rows. The close agreement is consistent with the recorded payments being
daily aggregates, although historical oracle prices remain unavailable.

For each path we remove daily-booked funding and add reconstructed hourly
payments only through formation or the official close, as appropriate.
After formation the frozen path uses fixed signed quantities. We deduct hourly funding before assessing the margin requirement. Table~\ref{tab:hourfund}
reports the result; closing costs and market impact remain excluded.
The volatility mean-square ratio becomes $F=\alFundingVolF$ and its rank
concordance is unchanged. Pooled prices may be hours old, especially when
equity markets are closed. The table assumes shared collateral. We consider isolated-margin
liquidations separately in Section~\ref{sec:orghourly}.
